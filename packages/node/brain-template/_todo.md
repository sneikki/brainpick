# Parking lot

Tasks that surface mid-work but fall outside its scope land here instead of
derailing the task at hand. Project management, not knowledge — that is why
this file lives beside the brain, not in it. Per-developer and gitignored —
never committed, never a merge-conflict magnet. Check it before planning
new work: it may already flag a known imperfection, a planned deprecation,
or something overlapping the task.
